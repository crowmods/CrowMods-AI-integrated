# CrowMods AI — Phase 49: Customer Support & Notification Engine

Adds a centralized support and lifecycle-notification foundation.

Capabilities:
- support tickets
- AI answer drafts
- ticket priority/classification
- customer notification preferences
- lifecycle notification templates
- notification queue
- delivery status
- escalation
- audit events

Notifications are opt-in and channel-aware. Production delivery should use
official provider APIs and respect consent, unsubscribe rules, rate limits and
applicable messaging laws.

AI drafts remain reviewable for sensitive or consequential support cases.

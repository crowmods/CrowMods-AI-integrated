# Phase 27 Notes

The strategy engine should eventually consume:
- campaign click-through rate;
- engagement rate;
- conversion rate;
- community growth;
- release views/downloads;
- platform-specific performance;
- previous experiment results.

Do not use:
- sensitive personal attributes;
- scraped private profiles;
- fake engagement;
- automated account creation;
- platform-rule evasion.

AI strategy should recommend and explain. Deterministic policy and the existing
approval workflow decide whether a campaign can actually be published.

For timing, use the platform/account's own historical performance rather than
assuming a universal "best time to post."
